About ME: Luzuko Menzipo

I started MenziCrack because I saw too many friends , family and colleagues in Cape Town struggling with back pain, neck stiffness and bad posture from sitting at desks all day. Instead of just replying on painkillers, I wanted to offer a more natural, hands-on way to help people feel better and move better.

The name "MenziCrack" comes from my surname "Menzipo" and the small "crack" sound during a spinal adjustment. My goal is to make chiropractic care feel friendly and not scary.

Qualifications:

To become a qualified chiropractor in South Africa, I completed roughly 6 years of university study:

  • 4 year Bachelor of Health Sciences (BHSc) in Chiropractic. Study areas: Anatomy , Physiology, Basic Sciences, Diagnosis
  • year Master of Health Sciences (MHSc) in Chiropractic Clinical training and patient care
  • Registered with: The Allied Health Professions Council of South Africa (AHPCSA)

Mission:

The core mission is to provide honest, healthy, hands-on chiropractic care that helps people in Cape Town and someday the rest of South Africa to get back to living without pain, so they can enjoy movement again.
